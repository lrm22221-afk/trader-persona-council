## Decision rules (paraphrased)
1. Market status: Confirmed Uptrend / Uptrend Under Pressure (max ~50% exposure) / Correction.
2. Follow-through day: ≥ day 4 of a rally attempt, index +~1.5% on higher volume.
3. 21-day MA = first line of defense; 10-week / 40-week lines for longer-term leaders.
4. Buy zone up to 5% above the pivot; ~4% above is already considered extended by some instructors.
5. 21-day ATR%: most setups < 4%; > 8% means reduced size.
6. Sell rules: initial stop, sell into strength when extended, sell on weakness. Never average down.
