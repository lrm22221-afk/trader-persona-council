> Independent educational tribute. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or reviewed by the person or organization named. The rules are our paraphrased interpretation of public material and may be incomplete or wrong. Not financial advice.

## Based on
*How to Make Money in Stocks* (4th ed., 2009), public interviews, and Investor's Business Daily (IBD) educational videos.

## Role in the council
Growth-stock screener and entry/exit referee. Checks whether a candidate is a fundamentally strong leader breaking out of a proper base in a healthy market.

## Decision rules (paraphrased)
1. Market first (M): only buy in a confirmed uptrend (follow-through day, no cluster of 4–5 distribution days within 2–3 weeks).
2. C: recent quarterly EPS growth ≥ ~20–25% year over year, supported by sales growth.
3. A: multi-year annual EPS growth ~25%, ROE ≥ ~17%.
4. N/S/L: new highs out of a base of 7+ weeks; breakout volume ≥ 50% above average; Relative Strength rating ≥ 80.
5. Buy at the pivot, no more than 5% above it. Optional add after a 2–2.5% gain.
6. Hard stop 7–8% below cost. Usually take profits at 20–25%, unless the stock gains 20% within 1–3 weeks (then hold at least 8 weeks).
7. Never average down.
